- Earlier approved native reference evidence: [Home](home-fidelity.md), [connected event/study](pilot-fidelity.md), and [remaining selected native](remaining-native.md). These do not by themselves verify the October 19 shared changes.
- Current results, exact test scope and corrections are recorded in [rollout-validation.md](rollout-validation.md). The [review gallery](previews/design-rollout.html) includes source capture manifests, appearance/size variants, and Home preservation comparisons.
- Real-device VoiceOver, real email/Apple account recovery/deletion, remote moderation writes, StoreKit lifecycle events and delivered trial reminders remain explicit external release checks. Mock/Debug fixtures cannot pass those gates.

## Deliberate native differences

Native inline back bars are retained on utility screens, and compact selected headers remain on approved families. Native Lists support preference editing/reordering. Quick Look, AVKit, alerts, Apple sign-in/purchase/subscription management and notification permission interfaces remain system-provided. Dark mode and accessibility layouts may wrap/stack/scroll instead of reproducing fixed reference heights.

## Future additions

Add a row for every new destination or presentation, choose a documented recipe, enumerate real states and entry paths, and link comparison/test evidence. Follow [screen-recipes.md](screen-recipes.md). Do not mark an entire flow verified because its root screenshot rendered.
